* What is left in this profile

The export machinery — one PDF beside the source, notes put in reading order on
a staged copy, heading depth, the redirect of =org-typst-export-to-pdf= — is
shared and lives in =base/export.org=. Two things are not shared, and this file
is both of them:

- **The language.** =org-export-default-language= decides hyphenation and, with
  the vendored patch, whether Typst is told =dir: rtl=.
- **Why it matters here.** Typst infers direction from the first strong
  character, and a numbered heading starts with a Latin digit: without
  =dir: rtl= a numbered Hebrew heading sits at the left margin while the text
  under it sits at the right.

The patch that emits =dir: rtl= for RTL languages is one of the four carried in
=vendor/ox-typst/upstream.patch=; =bin/check-export.sh= fails if a re-vendor
loses it, and =test/export-in-profile.el= fails if the hebrew profile stops
passing =#set text(lang: "he", dir: rtl)= to the exporter.

* Priced

0 ms: one variable assignment, read by the exporter when it runs.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
;; -*- lexical-binding: t; -*-

;; The document language, not the keyboard layout: it decides hyphenation and,
;; with the vendored patch, whether Typst is told `dir: rtl` so that a numbered
;; heading in a Hebrew sefer aligns to the right margin like the text under it.
(setq org-export-default-language "he")
#+end_src
